# Project Brief: Realm Raiders

## Project Overview
**Realm Raiders** (Project Realm Raiders) is an asymmetric fantasy realm management, continuous simulation, and asynchronous PvP catapult siege game. The entire experience is framed from the diegetic perspective of a medieval ruler seated at a grand royal desk, interacting with an illuminated, living parchment cartography map.

## Core Vision & Pillars
1. **Diegetic Immersion ("The Living Parchment")**:
   - Everything from city planning to war councils and siege combat takes place on antique sheepskin parchment with royal wax seals, calligraphic inks, quills, and real-time audio synthesis.
2. **Asymmetric Faction Strategic Matrix**:
   - Four lore-rich factions (Humans of Valor, Bloodfury Orcs, Sylvaeth Elves, Ironpeak Dwarves), each offering distinct macroeconomic yields, unique upkeep footprints, and asymmetric military siege advantages.
3. **Living Astrological Simulation & Meteorology**:
   - Dynamic in-game clock, 7-day seasonal rotations (Verdant Thaw, Sunfire Solstice, Golden Harvest, Frostveil Eclipse), weather fluctuations (Monsoon Deluge, Scorching Drought, Biting Gale), and population upkeep attrition.
4. **Asynchronous Catapult Siege & Blood Feuds**:
   - Matchmaking against generated rival realms. Players expend 3 heavy stone munitions in an interactive 2.5D raid view to plunder rival granaries and vaults, triggering retaliatory entries in the victim's Revenge Ledger.
5. **Zero-Asset Procedural Audio Synthesis**:
   - All sounds (coins, hot wax stamping, sword clashes, catapult launches, wall collapse) are synthesized on the fly via the browser's Web Audio API with zero external asset dependencies.
