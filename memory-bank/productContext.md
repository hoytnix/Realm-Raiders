# Product Context: Realm Raiders

## Why Realm Raiders Exists
Realm Raiders brings back the tactical charm of classic asymmetric strategy games (like Stronghold, Tribal Wars, and Clash of Clans) re-imagined as a tactile, tactilely satisfying, single-screen diegetic desk simulator ("The Living Parchment").

## User Experience & Metaphor
- **Throne Room POV**: The player is the supreme sovereign. The primary viewport is a royal cartographer's desk illuminated by flickering torches.
- **Parchment Cartography**: A 5x5 isometric settlement map drawn in sepia ink. Buildings animate with smoking chimneys, turning windmill blades, and active laborers.
- **Wax Seal Decrees**: Upgrades and major royal orders are executed by stamping hot wax onto parchment documents accompanied by synthesized thuds and sizzling wax audio.
- **Chronometer & Season Dial**: An astrological HUD displaying the hour, day, current season, weather tint, and simulation speed toggles (1x, 2x, 5x).
- **War Council & Retaliation Board**: A strategic war room tab presenting scouted rival lords, rating discrepancies, estimated plunder caches, and a dedicated Revenge Ledger for retaliatory strikes.
- **Catapult Siege Minigame**: An interactive 2.5D siege interface where players launch 3 heavy stone munitions at enemy fortifications (Keep, Granary, Vault, Lumber Mill, Ballista Watchtower) to plunder resources.
